This Week in AI Shorts & Video Tools
— June 2026, Week 1 —

📅 2026-06-05

The AI video space is moving faster than ever. This week, we're spotlighting five tools that are changing how creators in Japan, Korea, and beyond produce short-form video — from text-to-video generation to AI dubbing and automatic subtitles. Whether you're a VTuber, YouTuber, or TikTok creator, these tools will save you hours every week.

🏆 OpenAI Sora 2

💲 ChatGPT Plus $20/mo (limited) → Pro $200/mo | ⭐ Text-to-Video ★★★★★

Sora 2 is the most talked-about AI video generator of 2026. It generates up to 60 seconds of high-quality video from text prompts with dramatically improved physics and consistency over v1. For creators who need B-roll, background footage, or concept visuals without a camera, Sora 2 is the new standard. Available to ChatGPT Plus and Pro subscribers.

→ Try Sora 2

🎬 Runway Gen-4

💲 Free tier → Unlimited $95/mo | ⭐ Professional Video ★★★★★

Runway Gen-4 continues to be the go-to for professional-grade AI video. Its latest update brings frame-accurate editing, multi-shot scene generation, and improved text-to-video consistency. Creators are using it for everything from YouTube intros to full AI-generated short films. The free tier gives you enough credits to test before committing.

→ Explore Runway Gen-4

📱 CapCut AI Suite

💲 Free → Pro $9.99/mo | ⭐ All-in-One Creator Tool ★★★★★

CapCut remains the most practical AI video tool for short-form creators. Its AI suite now includes auto-captions (95%+ accuracy for Japanese and Korean), AI voiceover with natural TTS, background removal, and one-tap templates. The free tier is genuinely usable — no watermark, no time limit. If you make Shorts or TikToks, you need CapCut.

→ Use CapCut (Free)

⚡ Pika 2.0

💲 Free tier → Unlimited $28/mo | ⭐ Fast Animation ★★★★☆

Pika 2.0 has carved out a niche as the fastest AI animation tool. Where Sora and Runway excel at realistic footage, Pika shines with stylized, creative animations — perfect for music videos, lyric visualizers, and eye-catching Shorts hooks. Its "Pikaffects" feature lets you transform static images into dynamic clips in seconds.

→ Check Pika 2.0

🎙️ ElevenLabs AI Voice

💲 Free 10 min/mo → Pro $22/mo | ⭐ Voice & Dubbing ★★★★★

No AI video is complete without voice. ElevenLabs continues to lead in AI voice synthesis with natural Japanese and Korean TTS, voice cloning, and the Dubbing Studio (90+ languages). Pair it with any AI video generator above for a complete creator pipeline. The free tier gives you 10 minutes of generated audio per month.

→ Try ElevenLabs Free
